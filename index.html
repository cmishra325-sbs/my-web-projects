Frequently Asked Questions

Is Funngro safe for teenagers?

Absolutely. Funngro requires parental consent for all users under 18. All projects are reviewed for age-appropriateness, and every interaction is monitored. We are compliant with Indian child protection laws and take safety extremely seriously.

How do teens get paid?

Payments are transferred directly to the teen's bank account (via UPI or NEFT) within 7 days of project completion and approval. For minors, payments are routed through a parent/guardian-linked account.

Will this affect my child's studies?

Funngro is designed to be flexible. Teens can choose projects that fit their schedule, and most tasks require only 2-5 hours per week. Many parents report that the real-world application actually improves their child's academic motivation.

What kind of projects do companies post?

Projects range from social media content creation, product reviews, UI/UX feedback, survey participation, blog writing, video creation, to market research. All projects are designed to be age-appropriate and skill-building.

How much does it cost to join?

Funngro is completely free for teens to join. There are no hidden fees or charges. Companies pay a platform fee to post projects, but teens never pay anything to participate or earn.
